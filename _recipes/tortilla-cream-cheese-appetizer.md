## Ingredients
- 2 (8 oz) cream cheese
- 4 T mayo
- 1/2 C. green peppers, diced
- 4-5 tsp. chopped onions
- 1 can Hormel Chunked Ham, drained
- 1/2 tsp. garlic salt
- 9 tortillas (8-10 inches) — source uses 8 inch

## Instructions
1. Cream together cream cheese and mayo.
2. Stir in green peppers, onions, ham, and garlic salt.
3. Spread mixture on 6 tortillas. Layer tortillas, ending with 3 layers of 3 tortillas each (mixture between layers, top tortilla left plain).
4. Refrigerate overnight.

## Notes
- Source: Mary
- Usually substitute jalapeños for the green peppers.
- Can add pimento for color.
